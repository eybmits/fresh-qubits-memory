# Data

The current reproduction needs no external dataset. Its inputs are the manuscript's `q=0.9` and odd copy counts `r=1,3,...,15`.

For future datasets, record origin, retrieval date, checksum, license or access restrictions, schema, units and any preprocessing. Keep large or unreviewed inputs in ignored `data/raw/`; commit small manifests and scientifically relevant configurations. Do not commit credentials or personal data.
