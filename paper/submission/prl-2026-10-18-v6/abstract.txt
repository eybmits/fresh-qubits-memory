Fresh qubits, not size, set the memory ceiling of a noisy quantum reservoir. With k input qubits reinitialized per time step, the longest memory any reservoir can attain under depolarizing noise is exp[Θ(k)] time steps, whatever its size, dynamics, and input encoding. A universal upper bound and an explicit reservoir that attains the exponential scaling establish this optimal resource law. The bound rests only on the entropy budget of each time step, so it applies to any noisy quantum dynamics supplied with fresh qubits, from quantum reservoirs to measurement-free error correction.
